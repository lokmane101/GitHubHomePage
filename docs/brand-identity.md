# Lokmane Akkouh identity

The active logo is a folded abstract symbol. Three shaped pieces and an open diagonal passage suggest an A without setting the initials in a stock font. The SVG is editable: three closed filled paths, no fonts, strokes, filters, embedded images, or external resources.

## Usage

Forest ink is `#183c36`; celadon paper is `#e8ede2`. Use the reversed version on forest or another dark, plain surface. The portfolio retains its local General Sans and Satoshi fonts. The social image uses those supplied fonts; the symbol itself needs none.

Keep the square SVG canvas and natural proportions. Its internal margins provide clear space. At 16 px, use the tighter favicon asset; use the normal symbol at 32 px or larger. Do not crop, stretch, outline, add effects, or close the diagonal aperture. The maskable icon reserves extra surrounding space.

## Creation and verification

Visual exploration used the built-in image-generation tool with the `base-logo-generator` approach. The folded direction was rebuilt as SVG using the installed `logo-design` skill. Three principal straight edges were refined to a shared 60° angle; the shape was optically centered. The installed skill's SVG audit found no issues in the final master (100/100 heuristic score). This is an engineering check, not a substitute for visual judgment or trademark clearance.

PNG exports use the preinstalled Sharp/librsvg runtime. No new project dependency or build step was added. The symbol was inspected at 16, 24, 32, 48, and large sizes. Site references and declared image dimensions are checked with `scripts/check_site.py`; desktop, tablet, and 375 px layouts are inspected with Codex's browser tools. These checks do not certify automated accessibility conformance.

### Exploration prompt

Design a single professional logo concept comparison board for Lokmane Akkouh, an independent Data & AI Engineer. Art direction: sophisticated Swiss-meets-contemporary editorial identity; forest ink #183c36 on pale celadon #e8ede2. Exactly three large isolated symbols in an evenly spaced horizontal row, each with generous whitespace; no headings, labels, words, mockups or text. They should feel custom and recognizable rather than stock. Left: a sculpted abstract folded ribbon forming a single offset opening and a purposeful diagonal cut, precise flat shapes, subtle softened terminals, compact memorable silhouette. Middle: a highly custom lowercase 'a' letterform as a single geometric cutout symbol with a distinctive open aperture, no stock font appearance. Right: a precise modular abstract mark made from two interlocking curved angular bands expressing connected systems; must be extremely simple and work as a tiny favicon. Strictly flat one-color vector-like artwork, crisp hard contours, intentional negative space, no outlines, no shading, no gradients, no textures, no 3D, no decorative containers, no generic stock LA initials, no plain triangular A, no network nodes, no circuit boards, no AI stars, no infinite knot. The previous blocky LA logo was rejected as generic. These must look like identities designed by a premium independent branding studio. Wide board aspect ratio.
